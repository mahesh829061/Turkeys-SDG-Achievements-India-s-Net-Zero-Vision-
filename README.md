# 🌍 Sustainable Progress: Turkey’s SDG Achievements & India’s Net-Zero Vision

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a **data-driven comparative analysis** of **Turkey’s Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) progress (2005–2015)** and evaluates **India’s pathway to achieving Net-Zero emissions by 2070**.

Turkey is studied as a **successful transition case**, moving from **Cluster 2 to Cluster 1** with the **highest average SDG growth rate (2.36)** globally. The insights are then applied to assess **India’s climate commitments**, current emission trajectory, and policy gaps.

📊 The analysis combines **data visualization, policy evaluation, and future emission forecasting**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze **Turkey’s SDG performance (2005–2015)**
- Identify **key government policies** driving Turkey’s growth
- Evaluate **India’s Net-Zero 2070 feasibility**
- Analyze **India’s renewable energy and decarbonization initiatives**
- Recommend **strategic measures** using global best practices

---

## ❓ Problem Statement
Despite ambitious climate commitments, **India is currently not on track to achieve Net-Zero emissions by 2070**.  
This project investigates whether **Turkey’s SDG growth model** can serve as a **practical reference framework** for India’s sustainable development and climate transition.

## 🌍 Why Turkey?
- **Highest SDG growth rate** among all countries (2.36)
- Successfully transitioned from **Cluster 2 → Cluster 1**
- Similar starting position to India
- Strong focus on **healthcare, education, infrastructure, and R&D**

---

## 📈 Key Insights from Turkey (2005–2015)

### 🔹 Top Performing Indicators
- 📚 Increased school enrollment (especially female education)
- 🌐 Expansion of internet access
- 🧪 Growth in scientific research & innovation
- 🚰 Improved sanitation & food production

### 🔹 Key Policies Driving Growth
- **Green Card Program** – Free healthcare for low-income citizens
- **Universal Health Insurance (2008)** – Healthcare access for all
- **Education Reform (4+4+4 system)** – 12 years compulsory education
- **R&D Incentives** – TÜBİTAK grants & increased R&D spending
- **Infrastructure Investments** – Roads, tunnels, bridges
